About

My background is in aerospace and defense systems, where I spent more than 19 years working with complex technical equipment and mission-critical operations.

I started building my homelab in 2020 to gain practical experience with networking, Linux, virtualization, and storage. What began as a small network installation has grown into an environment where I can test configurations, deploy services, and work through infrastructure design problems.

Homelab Infrastructure

My homelab has gone through several major revisions as my interests and technical requirements have changed. The current environment uses pfSense for routing and firewall management, UniFi for switching and wireless, XCP-ng for virtualization, and TrueNAS for storage.

Current Infrastructure

Networking

Netgate 4100 running pfSense, UniFi managed switches and wireless, VLAN segmentation, and firewall policies.

Virtualization

Two AMD Ryzen 9 XCP-ng hosts with Xen Orchestra for virtual machine management.

Storage

Two AMD Ryzen 3 storage servers running TrueNAS SCALE with ZFS.

Infrastructure Services

NetBox, Uptime Kuma, UniFi Network Controller, Trilium, Docker, and Home Assistant.

Project History

2020: Initial network installation, 11U rack, pfSense firewall, managed switching, FreeNAS storage, and Linux systems.

2023: Network redesign with a Netgate 4100, UniFi switching and wireless, and a larger rack.

2024–Present: Migration to AMD Ryzen compute and storage hardware, XCP-ng virtualization, and TrueNAS SCALE.

Areas I'm Exploring

Advanced routing and switching, BGP, OSPF, private autonomous systems, network automation, and cloud infrastructure.
